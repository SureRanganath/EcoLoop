About EcoLoop

Closing the Loop on Industrial Waste

A circular economy marketplace for industrial waste exchange — powered by AI matching

What is EcoLoop?

One industry's waste, another's raw material

EcoLoop is a web-based industrial waste exchange that connects industries producing reusable waste materials with industries that can use those materials as raw inputs. Instead of paying to send scrap to landfill, a supplier lists it here — and a buyer who needs exactly that input can find it, get matched by AI, and complete the exchange on the platform.

Worked example: a plastics manufacturer with 500 kg of reusable HDPE scrap lists it at ₹45/kg. A pipe manufacturer needing recycled feedstock sees an AI recommendation scoring that listing on description, category, quantity, price and location — then submits a purchase request. The supplier accepts, the exchange is recorded, and both sides track it in their dashboard.

The Circular Economy

A circular economy keeps products and materials in use for as long as possible, extracting their maximum value before recovery and regeneration. It replaces the traditional linear model (make → use → dispose) with a loop:

Reduce

Minimise waste generation at the source by matching surplus to real demand.

Reuse

Find new applications for materials before they are shredded or melted down.

Recycle

Process materials into new products when direct reuse isn't possible.

AI Recommendation System

EcoLoop uses a content-based recommendation engine built with Python and scikit-learn. Every listing is scored against the buyer's request and each score is explained in plain language:

FactorWeightWhat it measures
Text similarity35%TF-IDF + cosine similarity between the request and the listing's title, category and description
Category match25%Exact category match, partial credit when no category is specified
Quantity15%Whether available quantity covers the requested amount
Location15%Preference for listings in the buyer's preferred state
Price10%Whether the price fits the stated budget, scaled by overage

A separate TF-IDF + Logistic Regression classifier suggests a material category from a free-text description. Both are content-based approaches suited to this dataset size — no pretrained-model accuracy is claimed, and every recommendation shows its reasoning.
